Since 2015

We started from zero.Twice.

Hack Arizona began in 2015, grew into the largest hackathon in the Southwest, went quiet when the pandemic hit, and came back in 2025. This is that story.

Our story, on film.

A short film about where Hack Arizona came from and the people bringing it back.

Coming soonThe film is being recorded.

Ten years, one signal.

  1. 2015

    It starts with students

    Students from academic units across the University of Arizona, including Computer Science, the School of Information, the Eller College of Management, the College of Fine Arts and the College of Social & Behavioral Sciences, start Hack Arizona.

  2. 2016

    The first hackathon

    The first Hack Arizona hackathon fills the room. It becomes a yearly fixture on campus.

  3. 2016 to 2020

    The biggest in the Southwest

    Year after year it grows, until Hack Arizona is the largest hackathon in the Southwestern United States.

  4. 2020

    The signal drops

    The COVID-19 pandemic brings Hack Arizona to a stop. The room goes quiet.

  5. 2025

    Back on

    Ten years after it began, Hack Arizona returns, rebuilt by students with the Division of Undergraduate Education: 100 participants, building again.

  6. 2026

    Stronger

    More than 180 participants at the Health Sciences Innovation Building, plus HackDay, our 7-hour mini hackathon: 52 students, 22 projects.

  7. 2027

    What comes next

    27 to 28 March 2027, at HSIB. Registration opens in January.
