What is Hack Arizona?

A hackathon is a time-bound event where people work together in teams to build a solution to a specific problem. Hack Arizona is the University of Arizona's student-driven hackathon, and the premier hackathon in the Southwestern United States.

Teams create projects that matter, pick up new skills and take on real-world challenges in technology and beyond. Every discipline is welcome, with hands-on workshops, speaker sessions and challenges built around real problems.

Students run it, and the Division of Undergraduate Education coordinates the planning across the whole university: undergraduates, graduate students, staff, faculty and administrators from every college.

Questions, answered.

When and where is Hack Arizona 2027?

27 to 28 March 2027, at the Health Sciences Innovation Building (HSIB) on the University of Arizona campus in Tucson (campus map). Start and end times will be announced here, in Arizona time (MST all year).

How do I register?

Registration opens in January 2027. Join the Discord or follow @hack.arizona to hear when it opens.

Who can take part?

Students at any institute of higher education in the state of Arizona, undergraduate or graduate, from any discipline.

Is it free?

Yes.

How many people can be on a team?

Two to four.

What should I bring?

Your laptop and chargers, a water bottle, a snack, comfortable clothes, your student ID card and an enthusiastic spirit.

Will food be provided?

Yes: lunch and dinner on the first day, breakfast on the second, and snacks through the first day and at midnight.

Is it overnight?

Yes, hacking runs overnight. There are no formal sleeping arrangements, but you may rest or sleep inside the venue.

What do the challenges look like?

In 2026 the challenges centred on themes like sustainability, AI, and outreach and marketing. The 2027 challenges and prizes will be announced closer to the event.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. Hack Arizona follows the MLH Code of Conduct.

Who can I ask?

Email the team at hackaz@arizona.edu.
