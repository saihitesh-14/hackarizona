Hack Arizona runs a flagship hackathon every spring and HackDays, mini hackathons, through the year. Here is what is coming up and what has happened so far.

  • Hack ArizonaThe flagship: 24 hours, once a year, students from every discipline.
  • HackDaysMini hackathons of 7 to 8 hours, through the year.

Hack Arizona AgX

Hacking the Future of Agriculture

  • Sunday 15 November 2026, 9:00 am
  • Grand Challenges Research Building
  • 7 hours

Hack Arizona partners with the Arizona Hub for Agricultural Innovation to host the AgX Arizona Summit. Challenges are revealed at kickoff, and the best teams pitch at the summit to producers, industry leaders, researchers and policymakers from ASU, the University of Arizona and NAU.

Who it is for: developers, designers, data scientists, engineers and anyone curious about agriculture. No farming experience is needed.

  • Real problem statements, revealed on the day
  • Mentorship from industry and university experts
  • Meals, swag and connections with Arizona's agtech community

What to know

Cost
Free.
Who
Students at any institute of higher education in Arizona.
Teams
2 to 4 people.
Bring
Your laptop, chargers, a water bottle, a snack, comfortable clothes and your student ID.
Food
Lunch and dinner on the first day, breakfast on the second, and snacks through the day and at midnight.
Overnight
It runs through the night. There is nowhere formal to sleep, but you can rest inside the venue.
