Hack Arizona

Motion prototypes for the new site

Two pieces built from the Hack Arizona mark: the logo as a vector that draws itself, and a 3D saguaro made of circuit traces. Both follow the same idea: a signal leaves the base of the trunk and lights everything it reaches.

The logo, as a vector

  • Traced from your circle logo PNG. Same shape and colours, now sharp at any size.
  • The draw-on is the site's loader idea: the ring powers on, the signal grows the saguaro from its base, then a pulse runs up and each pad lights up.
  • The last frame is the logo exactly as it is. Nothing is redrawn or recoloured.

Drag to turn it. Click it to send a signal.

The 3D circuit saguaro

  • Built from the logo's own measurements: trunk, arms and ring pads, with each arm turned into its own plane so it reads as a real cactus.
  • It grows in like the logo does, then turns slowly, floats, and leans toward your cursor. Flick it to spin it.
  • Every few seconds a signal runs up the traces and the pads light up.
  • Light theme uses the logo's red to navy gradient; dark theme turns it white with red signals.

Not in this prototype yet

  • Fonts, the hero layout and the rest of the page. These two pieces get dropped into the real hero.
  • Scroll linking (the saguaro turning as you scroll the hero) and the touch-device version.
