Veridise comes from veridise.com because the official page shows a thank-you banner
(https://hack.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2026-03/Veridise.png), not a logo. Veridise publishes its wordmark in
white only, so the dark-text copy is ours: ask Veridise for an official version for light backgrounds.

The other seven sponsors on the official page (Amazon Tempe Town Lake Campus, Nuclearn, ASUA, General Dynamics Mission
Systems, GitHub, Center for the Philosophy of Freedom, Responsible Artificial Intelligence) were already built from
Sai's own logo files in `03 Sponsors/Sponsor Logos`, which match or improve on the official page's copies.
